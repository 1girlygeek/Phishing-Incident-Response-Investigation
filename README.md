# Phishing Incident Response Investigation

## Project Overview

This project documents a simulated phishing incident investigation conducted as part of my cybersecurity studies.

The investigation focused on analyzing suspicious email activity, reviewing security logs using Splunk, identifying potential security risks, and recommending improvements to help prevent similar incidents.

The objective was not only to understand what happened but also to evaluate how an organization could improve its security controls and incident response procedures.

## Incident Background

The simulated incident involved a phishing email campaign that reached multiple employees within an organization.

The investigation examined email-related activity and available security logs to understand the scope of the incident, identify suspicious behavior, and evaluate potential organizational risks.

The incident demonstrated how phishing campaigns can expose organizations to security threats and why preventive security controls are essential.

## Investigation Methodology

### 1. Initial Investigation

Reviewed the incident scenario and identified the systems and logs relevant to the investigation.

### 2. Log Analysis

Used Splunk to examine available security events, investigate suspicious activity, and identify patterns associated with the phishing campaign.

### 3. Timeline Reconstruction

Developed a chronological timeline to document relevant events and establish how the incident unfolded.

### 4. Findings and Analysis

Reviewed the evidence to identify security concerns, potential business impacts, and gaps in existing security controls.

### 5. Recommendations

Developed recommendations to reduce phishing exposure and strengthen the organization's ability to detect and respond to future incidents.

## Business Impact and Security Improvements

A widespread phishing campaign can create significant operational and financial risks.

Potential consequences include:
- Unauthorized access to employee accounts
- Exposure of sensitive organizational information
- Business interruptions
- Financial losses associated with investigation and recovery
- Reputational damage

These impacts represent potential risks, not necessarily confirmed outcomes of this simulated incident.

### Recommended Security Controls

**Advanced Email Filtering**

Strengthen email filtering to detect suspicious messages, malicious links, and potentially harmful attachments before they reach employees.

**Phishing Awareness Training**

Provide regular employee training on recognizing suspicious emails, verifying senders, and reporting potential phishing attempts.

**Email Authentication**

Implement or strengthen SPF, DKIM, and DMARC to help protect against email spoofing.

**Multi-Factor Authentication**

Require MFA for organizational accounts to reduce the risk of unauthorized access if credentials are compromised.

**Security Monitoring**

Improve monitoring and alerting to identify suspicious email activity and potential account compromise.

## Lessons Learned

One of the most important lessons from this investigation was that cybersecurity is a shared responsibility.

Employees should be trained to recognize suspicious messages, but organizations must also implement technical safeguards that reduce the likelihood of malicious emails reaching users.

The investigation also reinforced the importance of maintaining accurate incident timelines, documenting evidence, and communicating findings clearly.

My background in production support and incident management provided a foundation for approaching this investigation systematically.

The same principles used to troubleshoot application failures, investigate production incidents, and identify root causes can be applied to cybersecurity incident response.
